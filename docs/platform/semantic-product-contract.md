# Semantic product contract

Every product eligible for Muse Shopping Index recommendations should carry decision-grade context.

## Required meaning

1. Product identity: what it is, brand/vendor, variant and legitimate identifiers.
2. Recipient fit: who it is appropriate for.
3. Occasion fit: when it is appropriate.
4. Semantic intents: natural-language shopping intents the product strongly satisfies.
5. Use cases: concrete situations or problems the product addresses.
6. Specifications: dimensions, materials, compatibility, package contents and other objective attributes when available.
7. Budget context: exact current price and variant boundaries.
8. Delivery context: inventory, dispatch evidence, destination support and confidence.
9. Bundle roles: anchor, complement, add-on or finishing touch.
10. Evidence: supplier/source data and timestamps behind claims.

## Recommendation eligibility

A product should not be eligible for BEST SIGNAL unless it is in stock, has a semantic completeness score of at least 80, has clear recipient or use-case context, and does not depend on unverified supplier claims for its recommendation.

Products with missing availability, questionable pricing, unsupported health claims, unclear compatibility or weak gifting context remain draft or rank below verified products.

## External ranking

The internal objective is top-1/top-2 relevance for contexts a product genuinely fits. External search or AI-shopping rank is never guaranteed. Optimization must not use fake reviews, keyword stuffing, invented identifiers, artificial scarcity or unsupported delivery promises.
