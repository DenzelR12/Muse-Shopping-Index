# Organic gift discovery campaign

## Catalog standard

Keep the public recommendation set between 20 and 50 products. A product enters the set only when it is authentic, currently available, photographed clearly, sensibly priced, and supported by a complete description, brand, variant, shipping context, and return context. Out-of-stock products and products with unclear authenticity stay out of recommendations.

The live Signal Standard collection contained 20 products when checked on September 16, 2026. Review it weekly and after supplier updates. Availability at checkout remains authoritative.

## Weekly publishing rhythm

Publish one Pinterest pin and one LinkedIn post for one buyer question each week. Link each post to the one guide or collection that answers that question. Use a real product photograph and keep the social title, page heading, and product intent aligned.

## Trust rules

- Publish reviews only after a verified order or documented customer experience.
- Never write or import a fabricated review.
- Keep price, availability, variants, and included items synchronized with Shopify.
- Send buyers to the relevant guide or product, not to an unrelated home page.
- State shipping and return terms clearly before checkout.
- Recheck supplier links, product images, and checkout paths every week.
- Record fulfillment delays and remove unreliable products from recommendations.
