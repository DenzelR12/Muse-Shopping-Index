What is real on this page, and what is not.

What about paying for the item in your hand and walking out?

That settles through the retailer's own point-of-sale, inventory and loss-prevention systems — a partnership, not an API. It is not part of this simulation and is not claimed. Mode 2 buys through the catalog: delivery, or pickup.
